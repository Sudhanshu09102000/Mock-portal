Questions

How do I check my typing speed?

Choose Speed test, pick 1, 2, 3 or 5 minutes and start typing the words shown. When time is up you see your WPM, KDPH, accuracy and the keys you miss most.

How is WPM calculated?

Every 5 correctly typed characters (spaces included) count as one word. WPM = correct characters ÷ 5 ÷ minutes. KDPH = correct characters per hour, so 35 WPM = 10,500 KDPH.

What is a good typing speed?

Below 25 WPM is a beginner, 30–40 WPM is average, 45–60 WPM is good and above 60 WPM is fast. For job and exam typing tests, accuracy matters as much as speed. Aim for 95% or more.

How can I learn typing fast as a beginner?

Learn touch typing: keep your fingers on the home row (A S D F – J K L ;), let each finger press only its own keys and avoid looking at the keyboard. Practise 30 minutes twice a day and use Weak keys mode to target your mistakes.
